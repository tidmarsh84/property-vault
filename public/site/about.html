Our Story

Solving a universal problem with a better way.

For decades, critical property information has been scattered across emails, filing cabinets, laptops and departments. When people move, buildings change or teams turnover, knowledge is lost and risk increases.

We built Property Vault to change that. Our platform connects every document, drawing, certificate and event in one intelligent, secure and permanent record—accessible anytime, anywhere.

Our promise:

To protect property information for today, empower decisions for tomorrow and preserve value for generations to come.
