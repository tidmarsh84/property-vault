Enterprise-Grade Security

Multi-layered protection for your property data

We combine cutting-edge technology, robust processes and rigorous compliance so you can store, share and manage property information with confidence.

AES-256 Encryption
All data is encrypted at rest and in transit using AES-256 encryption and TLS 1.3.
Sealed Immutable Records
Critical records are sealed and time-stamped to prevent tampering or unauthorised changes.
SHA-256 Fingerprinting
Every document is fingerprinted using SHA-256 to verify integrity and detect any change.
Role-Based Access
Granular roles and permissions ensure users only access what they are authorised to see.
UK Data Residency
All data is hosted in secure UK data centres. Your data stays in the UK.
Backups & Resilience
Automatic encrypted backups, geo-redundant storage and tested disaster recovery.
Monitoring 24/7
Continuous security monitoring, threat detection and incident response around the clock.
Audit & Accountability
Comprehensive audit logs track every access, change and action across the platform.
